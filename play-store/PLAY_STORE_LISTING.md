## Full description — English
My Deen is a free Islamic app for daily worship — no ads, no accounts, no payments.

• Prayer Times — accurate times for your city (JAKIM Malaysia method by default), live countdown to the next prayer, Hijri + Gregorian + Bengali dates, Suhoor & Iftar times, Tahajjud, Ishraq, Duha and Makruh times.
• Salah Tracker — mark each prayer done and build consistency with 7-day and monthly views.
• Holy Quran — all 114 Surahs in Arabic (Uthmani script) with Bengali translation, search, Surah/Ayah jump, bookmarks, and audio recitation from 8 famous Qaris with auto-play.
• Digital Tasbih — count your dhikr with targets (33/99/100/500/1000) and daily totals.
• Duas & Adhkar — authentic morning/evening, after-Salah, before-sleep and daily-life duas in Arabic, Bengali and transliteration. Ruqyah verses with audio.
• Qibla Compass — accurate Qibla direction from your location (291.9° from Ipoh, Malaysia).
• Fasting — Sunnah fasting reminders (Mondays & Thursdays, Ayyam al-Beed, Day of Arafah) with a Hijri calendar.

Your data stays only on your phone. Prayer times by Aladhan, Quran by AlQuran Cloud. Azan plays when the app is open; for background azan alerts, keep notifications on (Android).

Made with love for Muslims everywhere. 🤍
